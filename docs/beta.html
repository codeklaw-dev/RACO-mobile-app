Try the Raco beta on your phone

Raco runs inside the free Expo Go app on iPhone and Android. Setup takes about five minutes, and after that the same QR code always opens the newest build.

Open the beta

Finish the steps below first, then scan this code with your phone.

Already on your phone? Copy the link and paste it into Expo Go.

Set up, step by step

  1. Install Expo Go

    It's free. Use the latest version from your app store.

    App Store (iPhone) Google Play (Android)

  2. Accept your invite to the Raco team on Expo

    The beta is private. You'll get an email invite from Expo to join the Raco team. Accept it, and create a free Expo account if you don't have one. No invite yet? Ask whoever sent you this page.

  3. Sign in to Expo Go

    Open Expo Go, tap your profile, choose Log in, and use the same Expo account you accepted the invite with.

  4. Scan the QR code

    iPhone: point the Camera app at the code and tap the banner. Android: open Expo Go and tap Scan QR code. The first load takes up to a minute.

  5. Sign in to Raco

    Tap Sign up with email, enter any email address, then enter the code 123456. No real email is sent in the beta.

What to try

  • Chat: ask anything from the home screen. Under each reply you can copy it, hear it read aloud, save it to your Library, or report it.
  • Menu: tap ☰ or swipe in from the left edge. Long-press a chat to pin, rename or delete it.
  • Projects, Library and Daily tasks: all in the menu. Projects keep their own chats and tasks.
  • Voice mode: tap the round button in the message bar. Raco answers out loud.
  • Profile and Settings: tap your name at the bottom of the menu.
This is a demo build. Replies are samples, not real AI answers yet, and your chats and tasks reset when Expo Go restarts. In Expo Go you type to voice mode; talking to it needs the full Raco app.

Getting new versions

Each update is published to the same link. To get the newest one, close Raco completely in Expo Go (swipe it away) and open it again from Recently opened or this QR code.

If something goes wrong

"Not viewable in Expo Go: this project requires authentication"
You're not signed in to Expo Go, or your account hasn't joined the Raco team yet. Do steps 2 and 3, then scan again.
"Project is incompatible with this version of Expo Go"
Update Expo Go from the App Store or Google Play, then scan again.
You see an old version
Close Raco fully in Expo Go and reopen it.
The camera doesn't recognise the code
Open this page on your phone, tap Copy link, and paste it into your browser or Expo Go.
